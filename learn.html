Meru-Prastāra & Mātrā-Meru (Fibonacci in Sanskrit Meters)

Halāyudha's 10th-century commentary visualized Piṅgala's combinations as a mountain (Meru). Simultaneously, poets Virahāṅka and Hemacandra uncovered the exact sequence now called Fibonacci by enumerating poetic rhythms by total moraic time beats (mātrā).

Mātrā-Meru (Moraic Beats)

L = 1 beat, G = 2 beats. The count of valid meters with total duration M equals the (M+1)th Fibonacci number!
